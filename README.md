# Forensic-Aware_Multi-Task_Deep_Learning_Framework
A multi-task deep learning framework for forensic analysis of scanning electron microscopy (SEM) images, integrating morphological classification, material/device domain verification, image authenticity detection, and pixel-level manipulation localization. 
The framework combines complementary features extracted using EfficientNet-B0 and Vision Transformer backbones with forensic representations derived from residual, frequency-domain, local binary pattern, and error-level analysis maps. 
A gated feature fusion mechanism integrates these representations to support joint prediction, while a dedicated decoder localizes potentially manipulated image regions.
This work is significant because the integrity of SEM images is essential for the reliability and reproducibility of scientific and industrial research, yet image authenticity assessment and manipulation localization remain challenging for specialized microscopy imagery. In addition, we constructed a forensic SEM dataset by generating copy-move, splicing, synthetic-defect, and contrast manipulations from authentic images, enabling systematic evaluation of image-level classification and pixel-level localization. Comparative experiments and ablation studies investigate alternative CNN–Transformer combinations and the contribution of auxiliary domain and defect supervision to authenticity classification.
It should be of interest to readers in the areas of electronic imaging, image processing, computer vision, deep learning, scientific image analysis, and digital image forensics. We believe that the proposed integration of morphological analysis and forensic feature learning focus on imaging technologies and computational image analysis and offers a potential approach for improving the assessment of scientific image integrity.
